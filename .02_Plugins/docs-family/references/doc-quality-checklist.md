# Documentation Quality Checklist

## Accuracy

- Claims match code, tests, configs, schemas, or owner-approved docs.
- Version, environment, and platform constraints are stated.
- Unsupported behavior is not implied.

## Usability

- First-time reader can find the next action.
- Commands are copy-safe and scoped.
- Examples are minimal but realistic.

## Maintainability

- Ownership is clear where needed.
- Links point to durable source files.
- Repeated content is minimized or intentionally duplicated for usability.

## Safety

- No secrets, tokens, private keys, customer data, or unsafe credentials.
- Destructive operations have prechecks and warnings.
- Production operations have escalation and rollback paths.
