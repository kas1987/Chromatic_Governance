# Troubleshooting Template

```markdown
# Troubleshooting Guide

## Fast triage

What changed, who is affected, where it fails, severity.

## Issue: Symptom title

### Symptoms

Visible error, logs, behavior, or screenshot description.

### Likely causes

Ranked list of probable causes.

### Checks

Safe commands or inspection steps.

### Fix

Step-by-step remediation.

### Verify

How to confirm recovery.

### Escalate when

Conditions requiring owner, security, release, or incident escalation.
```
