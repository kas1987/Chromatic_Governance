# Roadmap

## Mission

Create a realistic, outcome-driven roadmap that sequences work by value, risk, dependency, and learning.

## Inputs to collect

- Product goal and time horizon
- Candidate features, fixes, platform work, and research
- User segments and business outcomes
- Dependencies, constraints, and known dates
- Capacity assumptions and release cadence
- Required format: now/next/later, quarterly, milestone, or theme-based

## Procedure

1. Anchor the roadmap to outcomes, not a feature wish list.
2. Group work into themes or milestones.
3. Sequence high-learning and high-risk work early.
4. Place dependencies before dependent features.
5. Leave room for QA, docs, security, release, and feedback loops.
6. Mark confidence level per milestone.
7. Include decision gates where evidence should update the plan.

## Guardrails

- Do not present speculative dates as commitments.
- Do not overload early phases with every dependency.
- Do not hide risk, staffing assumptions, or unknowns.
- Prefer flexible ranges unless the user has confirmed delivery dates.

## Expected output

- Roadmap frame and assumptions
- Themes or milestones
- Sequenced plan
- Dependencies and risks
- Decision gates
- Deferred items
- Next planning actions
