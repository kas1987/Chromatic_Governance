# Feedback Loop

## Mission

Turn raw feedback into evidence-weighted product decisions and actionable follow-up work.

## Inputs to collect

- Feedback source and timestamp
- Customer/user segment
- Exact quote, signal, metric, or behavior when available
- Frequency, severity, and business impact
- Related product area, release, or experiment
- Existing backlog items or decisions, if any

## Procedure

1. Preserve raw evidence separately from interpretation.
2. Group feedback into themes, not just individual requests.
3. Classify each signal as pain, desire, confusion, bug, churn risk, conversion issue, or opportunity.
4. Estimate evidence strength: anecdote, repeated signal, quantified trend, or validated pattern.
5. Map themes to possible product actions.
6. Create backlog items with acceptance notes, or recommend discovery if evidence is weak.
7. Update roadmap implications and open questions.

## Guardrails

- Do not treat loud feedback as representative without frequency or segment context.
- Do not erase minority but severe pain points.
- Do not convert every request into a feature.
- Preserve quotes accurately and label paraphrases.

## Expected output

- Feedback summary
- Themes and evidence strength
- User/business impact
- Recommended actions
- Backlog candidates
- Discovery questions
- Roadmap implications
