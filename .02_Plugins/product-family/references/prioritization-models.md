# Prioritization Models

## Fast impact/effort

Score impact and effort from 1 to 5.

Priority score = impact / effort, adjusted for risk and confidence.

## RICE

Reach x Impact x Confidence / Effort.

Use for product bets where audience size and confidence matter.

## MoSCoW

- Must: required for launch or safety
- Should: important but not launch-blocking
- Could: valuable if cheap
- Won't: intentionally excluded for this horizon

## Risk-adjusted priority

Use for platform, security, migration, or operational work.

Score:

- Impact if fixed or built
- Risk if delayed
- Effort
- Confidence
- Dependency unlock value

## Output rule

Always include assumptions. A bad score with visible assumptions is better than fake precision.
