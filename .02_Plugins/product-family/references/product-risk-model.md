# Product Risk Model

## Risk categories

| Risk | Question |
|---|---|
| Value risk | Does anyone meaningfully need this? |
| Usability risk | Can target users use it without confusion? |
| Feasibility risk | Can we build and operate it safely? |
| Viability risk | Does it make sense for the business/model? |
| Trust risk | Could this harm privacy, safety, compliance, or user confidence? |

## Severity

- P0: Blocks launch or creates unacceptable harm.
- P1: High chance of poor outcome or rework.
- P2: Manageable risk with mitigation.
- P3: Low risk or polish concern.

## Mitigation rule

Every P0/P1 risk needs an owner, mitigation, and validation path before implementation proceeds.
