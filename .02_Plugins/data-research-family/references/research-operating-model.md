# Research Operating Model

## Purpose

Use research to support a decision, not to accumulate links. Every research task should name the decision, claim, implementation question, product question, or risk being investigated.

## Research flow

1. Define the question and required freshness.
2. Identify source classes needed.
3. Collect a broad candidate set.
4. Rank by authority, relevance, recency, and bias risk.
5. Extract claims only where directly supported.
6. Synthesize themes and contradictions.
7. Produce confidence labels and handoff actions.

## Evidence labels

- Fact: directly supported by source.
- Inference: reasoned conclusion from one or more facts.
- Assumption: plausible but not established.
- Conflict: sources disagree or evidence points in multiple directions.
- Gap: evidence needed but not found.
