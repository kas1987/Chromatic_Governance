# Confidence Scale

| Level | Meaning | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| High | Strong, current, directly relevant evidence from authoritative sources | Multiple primary or authoritative sources align |
| Medium | Good but incomplete evidence | Some source limitations, minor conflicts, or weaker recency |
| Low | Directional only | Anecdotal, stale, vendor-biased, narrow, or indirect evidence |
| Unknown | Insufficient evidence | Research did not locate enough support |

Always explain what would raise or lower confidence.
