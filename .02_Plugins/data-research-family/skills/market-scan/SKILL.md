# Market Scan

## Mission

Map the market landscape and translate signals into strategic options.

## Inputs to collect

- User question, claim, decision, or comparison target
- Required timeframe and freshness requirements
- Required source types or excluded sources
- Decision context and downstream consumer
- Known constraints, versions, geography, platform, or workload
- Existing notes, links, files, or citations

## Procedure

1. Define category, geography, customer segment, and time horizon.
2. Identify competitors, substitutes, open-source alternatives, and adjacent workflows.
3. Collect evidence on positioning, pricing, features, customers, traction, and constraints.
4. Separate observed facts from inferred strategy.
5. Map user buying criteria and switching barriers.
6. Identify gaps, white space, and likely risks.
7. Produce strategic implications for product or GTM handoff.

## Guardrails

- Do not present weak web presence as proof of weak business.
- Do not over-index on vendor websites.
- Mark inferred revenue, share, or traction as estimates unless sourced.
- Avoid legal, investment, or purchasing certainty without current authoritative verification.

## Expected output

- Market definition
- Competitor/substitute map
- Signal table
- Positioning themes
- White space
- Risks
- Product/GTM implications
